## Product purpose

Life Pilot is a personal life-navigation platform built to help people understand how the connected parts of life affect one another, especially where social and economic pressure is present.

It brings money, work, home, family, future decisions, documents, people, providers, obligations and activity into one practical view. It helps a person see what matters, understand how a smaller decision may affect a larger outcome, and take the next practical step without shame or overwhelm.

Life Pilot is not merely a task manager, budgeting app, case-management system or AI chatbot. Its purpose is to improve practical understanding and decision-making across real life.

> For when life throws a whole lot of life at you.

> See what matters. Plan what's next. Take the next practical step.

Theme: **Life Pilot - Real Life Signal.**

## Primary commercial customer

The primary commercial customers are organisations that support people experiencing social and economic pressure:

- Government and local government.
- NGOs and not-for-profits.
- Community organisations and social enterprises.
- Employment, housing, financial wellbeing and family-support services.
- Place-based and cohort-funded programs.

The end user is the person or household navigating real life. Partner access must not become surveillance. Sharing must be user-controlled, consent-based and limited to what is necessary.

## Product proposition

Life Pilot helps users and trusted support contexts:

- Bring scattered life information into one trusted view.
- Understand the relationship between money and wider life decisions.
- Identify pressure early enough to preserve options.
- Learn how smaller choices can influence larger outcomes.
- Compare a controlled set of practical scenarios.
- Move forward through a clear next practical step.

Money is a foundational signal across the platform. It links to each Life Area rather than operating as a separate top-level bucket.

## Locked product hierarchy

### Level 1 - Life Pilot

The umbrella personal life-navigation platform.

### Level 2 - Life Pilot Starter

The always-on commercial core and current MVP focus.

### Level 3 - Four Core Life Areas

These are the only default top-level Life Areas and the basis of the four-dash GPS motif:

1. **Home Life** - household administration, bills, documents, appointments, routines, services and everyday obligations.
2. **Work Life** - employment, job search, earning activity, work evidence, reporting and income-linked decisions.
3. **Family Life** - household members, chosen family, caring responsibilities, trusted people, communication and support relationships.
4. **Future Life** - goals, controlled scenario planning, pressure periods, option comparison and future decisions.

Do not add Money Life or Support Life as default top-level buckets. Money and support operate across the four Life Areas.

### Level 4 - Life Tools

Shared tools operating across the four Life Areas. They are not additional Life Areas.

Current examples:

- Activity.
- Calendar overlay.
- Documents and evidence.
- People and providers.
- Shopping and budget checks.
- Notifications.
- Control Centre.

### Level 5 - Activated Layers

Conditional layers that appear only when onboarding, pressure signals or user consent make them relevant.

- **Real Life** - activated when life becomes unusually complex, pressured or disrupted. Positioning: "Support for when life gets very real."
- **Business Life** - activated for relevant self-employment or business contexts.

Activated layers are not default buckets and are not part of the current MVP build priority.

## Life Signal and GPS

The Life Signal converts information across the four Life Areas and Life Tools into a clear, explainable view of:

- What matters now.
- What needs attention.
- What may happen next.
- What practical action can be taken.

Signals must be understandable and traceable. Life Pilot must not present unexplained scores, mystical prediction or unsupported certainty.

The GPS interaction model is:

- Get clear.
- Plan ahead.
- Step forward.

The four-dash motif must use the approved GPS colours, order, proportions and visual language consistently.

### Basic scenario planning

Basic scenario planning already exists and is part of MVP.

It uses a fixed library of common scenarios with limited adjustability. Users may change a small number of defined inputs and view the effect on their position, priorities and next practical steps.

The MVP scenario capability is structured, rules-led, explainable and repeatable. Before an AI layer is added, Life Pilot must establish which model can produce dependable signals consistently. AI may later assist interpretation, but it must sit on top of a validated and explainable model.

## Evidence and reliability requirements

Before stronger claims or advanced AI are introduced, Life Pilot must test:

- Whether users understand each signal.
- Whether the same inputs produce consistent outputs.
- Which inputs materially affect a scenario.
- Whether the next practical step is useful and proportionate.
- Whether users understand the relationship between money and wider life decisions.
- Whether language remains respectful and non-shaming.
- Whether partner use protects consent, privacy and agency.
- Which customer and funding model provides the strongest sustainable pathway.

## Brand and language guardrails

Life Pilot must remain warm, grounded, practical, mature, inclusive, human, respectful and capable.

Use warm sand and near-white surfaces, deep slate serious content, deep teal primary actions and signal orange only for next actions and important attention markers. Use the approved GPS colours and simple mature icons.

Avoid cockpit, control-tower, captain, plane, wings or airline language; cartoon icons; charity clichés; large rainbow blocks; generic SaaS or productivity-bro language; mystical prediction; and presenting Real Life as the default experience.

## Investor and partner framing

People facing social and economic pressure often manage interconnected issues across systems that do not communicate with each other. A small decision can produce a larger effect across money, work, home, family and future options.

Life Pilot helps make these connections visible and supports learning, practical action and better-informed choices.

The current commercial wedge is organisation-supported access and testing with government, NGO, NFP, community and social-enterprise partners. Direct-to-user pathways remain possible but are not the sole or primary framing.

Claims must remain evidence-led. Life Pilot does not replace professional financial, legal, health or social support.
